## 7. Open items

**Post office: held** (2026-10-27). Enter, menus, talk, and leave all work, and the after-leave black screen is fixed (10+ enter/leave cycles confirmed by the operator). The black screen was never an exit or a hang: the render loop stayed healthy and fast while the display-list buffer gave out. Root cause: `Player_actor_ct_other_func1` (`src/game/m_player_other_func.c_inc`) spawns a `mAc_PROFILE_FIELD_DRAW` actor with every player-actor construction — retail plays die with the scene so it never mattered, but the persistent town play accumulated one per interior visit (the whole map drew every frame, 126 blocks). Fixes, all playtested: spawn the field-draw actor only when the play has none; `aMHS_guest_walking_out` now also requires the player within 60 units of that house's spawn point (house actor position + the house-side diagonal used by `Actor_info_resident_door` — the spawn point sits ~40 units past the retail door-trigger radius, so joins needed the wider anchor); and `pc_world_transition` resets the town's `next_scene_no` to its own scene and clears `s_enter_slot` after every completed enter/leave. The join's house-door animation regression was the same anchor mistake, fixed and retested. All probes from this hunt are removed; `crash_log.txt` and its SEH filter stay per audit A8. Then the museum, then the map board. Safe return `aee5c2f`. Held tools are second last. 3-4 players are last. Frame pacing and multiplayer audio are in `tasks.md` Later; the frame limiter disengaging after the first interior leave (16.7 ms -> 0.1 ms) is recorded there too.

**Ghost model on load: fixed (2026-10-25, playtested).** A P1-shaped model appeared on the ground at P1's door for about two seconds after loading with Player 2 active. Every player actor runs `Player_actor_ct` → DMA → `Player_actor_request_proc_index_fromDma`, which reads `actor_specific & 7`: 1-3 request `mPlayer_INDEX_OUTDOOR` (the `mPlayer_ANIM_GO_OUT_O1` "walk out of a doorway" state plus an `mDemo_TYPE_OUTDOOR` request). `Actor_info_spawn_guest` passed the host's `player_data->arg`, so the guest replayed P1's house exit at P1 + 80 x, 80 units into the open where no door hides it; single player never sees the host's own version. Playtest: no model at the door, P2 stands normally, P1 controllable sooner. Probes are gone.
